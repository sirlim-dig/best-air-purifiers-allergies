Frequently Asked Questions

Do air purifiers actually help with allergies?

Yes — if they use True HEPA filtration. HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns, including pollen, dust mite allergens, pet dander, and mold spores. Multiple clinical studies show HEPA air purifiers reduce allergy symptoms by 40-60%.

What size air purifier do I need?

Match the purifier's coverage area to your room size. For bedrooms (150-300 sq ft), the Levoit Core 300S or Blueair Blue 3210 work great. For living rooms (400-800 sq ft), consider the Winix 5500-2. For whole-floor coverage, the Coway Airmega 400 handles up to 1,560 sq ft.

How much do replacement filters cost?

Expect $30-60 per filter, replaced every 6-12 months. The Levoit Core 300S has the cheapest filters (~$20-25). The IQAir HealthPro Plus has the most expensive (~$80 for the HEPA filter every 18 months). Factor this into your total cost of ownership.

Should I run my air purifier 24/7?

Yes, for best results. Modern purifiers on low settings use only 5-15 watts — about $5-15 per year in electricity. The continuous filtration keeps allergen levels consistently low rather than letting them build up.
